Quick answers

Where is my information stored?

Schedules, preferences, and activity are stored locally in Chrome. Tabby has no account system or remote data service.

Why did a scheduled page not open?

Confirm the schedule is active, the start time is in the future, and Chrome is running. Overdue launches are safely marked as missed instead of opening unexpectedly later.

How do I stop a schedule?

Pause it from the dashboard to keep it for later, or delete it permanently. You can also set an optional final run date.

How do I remove activity history?

Use Clear log in the activity panel or Settings. You can also choose how many days activity is retained.
