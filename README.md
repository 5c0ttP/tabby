## What is Tabby?

Tabby is a simple Chrome extension that opens websites for you at the times you choose.

Want your email to open when work starts? A workout video every evening? A weekly report every Friday? Give Tabby the website, pick a time, and it takes care of the rest.

## How it works

1. **Add a website** — Paste the link you want Tabby to open.
2. **Choose a time** — Pick a date, time, and whether it should repeat.
3. **Let Tabby handle it** — The page opens automatically, right on schedule.

You can also ask Tabby to close the page after a set amount of time. It only closes the exact tab it opened—never your other tabs.

## Made for everyday routines

- Open a morning dashboard when your day begins
- Bring up a meeting link just before the call
- Start a focus playlist at study time
- Remember weekly forms, reports, and timesheets
- Open reading, exercise, or learning pages on a routine

## Simple, flexible, and private

| | |
| --- | --- |
| 🗓️ **Flexible schedules** | Once, every day, on weekdays, weekly, or on days you choose |
| ⏱️ **Optional auto-close** | Close a Tabby-opened page after a set duration |
| ⏸️ **Easy controls** | Pause, edit, run, or remove any schedule |
| 🌓 **Light and dark modes** | A comfortable dashboard at any hour |
| 🔒 **Private by design** | Your schedules and history stay on your device |

Tabby requires no account, contains no ads or analytics, and does not send your browsing activity anywhere.
